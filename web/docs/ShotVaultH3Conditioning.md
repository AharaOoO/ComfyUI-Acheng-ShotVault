# ShotVault H3 Conditioning · 分镜多模参照编码

**English** | Dynamically encodes the current shot's multimodal references (`<Picture 1..9>`, `<Video 1..3>`, `<Audio 1..3>`) plus optional direct canvas overrides into MiniMax H3 conditioning.
- In Audio-Driven mode, automatically mutes `<Audio>` voice refs and reference-video audio tracks to prevent lip-sync / rhythm conflicts.

---

**简体中文** | 按当前镜头动态编码在分镜规划台上传的多模态参考素材（`<Picture 1..9>`、`<Video 1..3>`、`<Audio 1..3>`）及画布直连扩展参考。
- 音频驱动模式下自动屏蔽 `<Audio>` 音色参考与参考视频原声，防止与母带/口型音轨冲突。
